About

The Universe is like an ocean, and we're only at the shore. Hi! I am an undergraduate student in Engineering Physics at the Indian Institute of Technology Hyderabad (2023–2027), pursuing a minor in Mathematics and Honors in Physics. My research interests lie in cosmology, gravitational-wave astronomy, black holes, dark matter and dark energy, neutron stars, and astro-particle physics. My current work spans gravitational-wave population studies, high-energy neutrino analyses using IceCube observations, galaxy-galaxy weak lensing, and cosmological simulations.

I am particularly interested in using observational data, statistical inference, and computational methods to investigate fundamental questions in astrophysics and cosmology.
